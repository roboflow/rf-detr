!!! success "Commercially usable: Apache 2.0"

    RF-DETR is released under the Apache 2.0 license, so it's commercially usable out of the box: you can ship it in closed-source, commercial products, including self-hosted deployments, without paying a license fee or open-sourcing your code. This covers the `rfdetr` package, RF-DETR Nano, Small, Medium and Large detection, every RF-DETR-Seg size (Nano through 2XLarge), and RF-DETR Keypoint (Preview).

    RF-DETR Atto, Femto, Pico, XLarge and 2XLarge detection ship in the optional `rfdetr[plus]` extension and use the [Roboflow Platform Model License (PML 1.0)](https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr_plus/blob/main/LICENSE): commercial use is included when you deploy on Roboflow, and commercial self-hosting requires the Enterprise add-on ([details](https://roboflow.com/licensing)).
